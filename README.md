# FUTURE_PE_03 — AI SEO Blog & Content Cluster Generator

> **Prompt Engineering Track | Future Interns 2026 | Task 3**

---

## Overview

This repository contains a complete AI-generated **SEO Blog & Content Cluster Pack** for a **Digital Marketing Agency in Hyderabad** — built using structured prompt engineering on Claude and ChatGPT.

The system follows how real SEO agencies plan content: one strong pillar blog supported by multiple cluster blogs, all keyword-focused and internally linked.

---

## Business Chosen

**NovaSpark Digital** — A fictional digital marketing agency based in Hyderabad, India.  
Services: SEO, Social Media Marketing, Google Ads, Content Marketing, Website Design.

**Why this niche?**  
Digital agencies have high search competition, making SEO content strategy extremely valuable. The Hyderabad market is booming with startups and local businesses seeking digital services.

**Primary Keyword:** `digital marketing agency in Hyderabad`  
**Target Audience:** Small business owners, startups, and entrepreneurs in Hyderabad looking for digital marketing services.
